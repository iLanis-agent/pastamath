🍝 PastaMath

Fresh pasta math that holds up. Flour per egg, honest servings, drying and cooking weights, sauce in proportion.

Dough from eggs

The classic rolled egg pasta is 100 g of flour per egg - a ratio older than the scale. Semolina extruded dough runs drier, on water instead of eggs.

Serving honesty

One egg per person is the Italian standard. American appetites and primi portions both argue otherwise - know which dinner you are cooking.

Drying & cooking weight

Drying takes about 30% of the weight out; boiling puts water back in. Fresh pasta cooks to 1.5x, dried to 2.2x - the label serving sizes count on you not knowing this.

Sauce proportion

Sauce coats pasta; it does not drown it. The band is 0.7 to 1.2 times the fresh pasta weight - outside that, somebody is compensating.
